### Completed

- Paired Wilcoxon speed comparisons performed.
- Holm correction applied.

### Main findings

1. Kinematic signals closely reproduce the nominal movement frequencies.
2. RMS differs among hand tasks in both EDC and FDS.
3. RMS systematically increases at the faster 0.75 Hz condition.
4. Median frequency differs among tasks.
5. FDS median frequency shows the strongest task-related effect among the
   currently evaluated descriptors.
6. Movement speed is therefore an important factor that future movement
   estimators should handle explicitly.
